Cantonese IME 1.0.0 for macOS

Native Cantonese input with the same offline dictionary, numbered candidates,
Yale/Jyutping typing, and local phrase learning as the Windows release.

Requires macOS 13 or later. The universal2 application includes native Apple
Silicon and Intel code and its own private Python runtime. Python, Homebrew,
and developer tools are not required on the receiving Mac.

The installer copies only CantoneseIME.app to /Library/Input Methods. It
does not change your default keyboard, add login items, or enable an input
source on your behalf. After installing, add Cantonese IME from the Keyboard
settings for your own account. You may need to sign out and sign in first.

Upgrading: switch to another input source and quit Cantonese IME first.
Other signed-in users must do the same or sign out. The installer does not
force applications to quit. Personal learning is stored separately and kept.
