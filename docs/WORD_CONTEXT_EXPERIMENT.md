# Fixed word-context experiment

This isolated experiment is complete and fails eligibility. It remains disabled
in production. The published 1.0.8 installer and tag are unchanged.
The [aggregate diagnostic](self-improvement-cycle4-word-diagnostic.json) records
the fixed policy, resource scope, all quality predicates and evidence hashes.
Raw pools, per-request gains/losses and new development labels remain private.

The provider uses 96,304 original HKCanCor word occurrences, 5,591 word types
and 34,610 adjacent-word types from the same frozen 39-group training whitelist.
HKCanCor is CC-BY-4.0, by K.K. Luke and May L.Y. Wong, hosted by Francis Bond.
No development or reserved token counts were fitted. All corpus groups were
previously exposed during this project; this is not an untouched corpus test.
Words retain raw written identity, with one unknown bucket. Smoothing remains
delta 0.1 / tau 10. Each composed arc adds one word-transition cost at coefficient
0.5 to its existing lexical/spelling cost; character scoring is off. Spelling,
direct-word priorities, dictionary data and numeric limits are unchanged.

The bounded word search passed 12,288 independent mechanical comparisons on
513 synthetic graphs, including expected capacity outcomes. An exact prefix
dominance rule resolves the 18 hypothesis-limit failures in the preserved first
word-search prototype; 9,846 previously completed pools remain identical.
Independent reconstruction matched every original unigram and word pair.
The immutable reader passed 68,161 exact probability/cost comparisons and
cached-file tamper checks. These checks establish mechanics and data integrity.

The pilot and development stages completed all 1,041 attempts without errors,
capacity failures, truncation or constraint violations. All 694 baseline/zero
full-pool, float-hex score, page and rank comparisons match the saved controls.
The 179 primary phrases count each proper compact-Yale phrase once; alternate
spellings in the 328-request development plan are reported separately.

The old 89 requirements remain at least 81 first-page hits and 85 first90 hits.
Word 0.5 reaches 25/29, 23/30 and 22/30 first-page hits by increasing length band;
the latter two bands require 24 each. Three nasal-control form requests lose
first-page coverage against each control. Five distinct new development
phrases enter the first90 from outside both controls, but those gains do not
override the failed old-case and individual nasal-control gates. This word
model also underperforms the earlier character model's 79/89 old first-page hits.

No model is selected, and protected validation remains unqueried. Actual
personalized histories, constituent learning, shorter/full-input preferences,
restart/cache behavior, controlled changing-input latency and native Windows
x64 TSF/application acceptance remain separate unfinished requirements.
Shared-process lookup clocks do not establish native speed or latency.

Reading accuracy has a separate regression: the old 89 exact text-plus-reading
first90 count falls from 70 to 68. Across all 328 development requests, each
control comparison has 151 text-rank gains and 46 losses, and 125 reading-rank
gains and 43 losses. These count requests and include spelling variants; they
are not additional distinct phrases. The independent saved-only assessment
reconciles all selectors, gates, signed changes and controls.
